Privacy Policy

JobSearch OS · Last updated: 4 November 2026

JobSearch OS ("the app") is a personal job search assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. It is used by one person, its owner, and is not offered to the public. This policy explains what Google user data the app accesses and what it does with it.

Data the app accesses

The app connects only to its owner's own Google account, using these Gmail API scopes:

  • gmail.readonly: to read the owner's messages related to job applications, such as replies from employers and recruiters.
  • gmail.send: to send application emails that the owner has reviewed and approved one by one.

How the data is used

  • Email content is used only to track the status of the owner's job applications and to show the owner new replies.
  • Sending is switched off by default. When it is on, the app sends only messages that the owner has approved one by one.
  • Google user data is not sold and is not used for advertising. The app itself does not use it to train AI or machine learning models.

Where the data goes

  • Owner's computer. Data is stored in a local database and local backups on the owner's own computer. Access tokens are kept in the operating system's keychain.
  • Telegram. To notify the owner about a new email, the app sends an excerpt of it (up to about 2,200 characters) to the owner's private chat with the app's Telegram bot. These messages pass through Telegram's servers.
  • Anthropic. The app analyses vacancies with Anthropic's Claude models, through Claude Code running under the owner's own Claude account. If the owner confirms that an employer's email changes a vacancy's terms, a short excerpt of that email (up to 350 characters) is saved with the vacancy and is sent to Claude together with it during analysis. That processing follows the data settings of the owner's Claude account.
  • Google user data is not shared with anyone else.

Retention and deletion

There is no automatic deletion: data is kept until the owner deletes it. The owner can delete the app's local data at any time and can revoke the app's access to the Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
